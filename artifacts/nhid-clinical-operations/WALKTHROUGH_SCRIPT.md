# Shadow Pilot CRM Walkthrough Script

## Opening

“Welcome to Shadow Pilot CRM, the NHID-Clinical operations workspace. This dashboard brings partner delivery, email review, knowledge, evidence, training, content, competitive intelligence, and administration into a single controlled operating surface.”

## Command Center

“We start in the Command Center. The top metrics summarize active Shadow Pilots, inbox items awaiting human review, evaluated calls, and the current control pass rate. The assurance trend visualizes the operational control signal across the recent evaluation cohorts. The review queue brings the highest-priority inbound messages into view, while the pipeline and today’s operations panels show where delivery work is moving.”

## Public Partner Intake and Shadow Pilot CRM

“New prospective partners enter through the public Shadow Pilot intake form. It captures the organization, primary contact, platform, estimated call volume, and use case. Submissions begin in the exact **Applied** stage.”

“In the Shadow Pilot CRM, partners progress through Applied, Vetted, Contract Sent, Integrated, Live, Reporting, and Complete. Each partner record groups contact details, integration readiness checks, call-volume evidence, a communication timeline, a raw-event view, and a violation trend for IDG-01, PDX-01, DBC-01, EIT-01, and ATR-01.”

## Email Command Center

“The Email Command Center classifies incoming communications into the approved categories: Pilot Inquiry, Pricing, Integration, Consulting, Media, Investor, Research, Vendor, and General. Each record has a confidence score and a review state.”

“When an operator chooses Generate AI Draft, the server prepares a knowledge-grounded response using the approved Shadow Pilot process, controls, pricing, and integration context. It is placed in Review. The system does not auto-send email; an operator can edit the content and explicitly choose Send after review.”

## Knowledge Base Engine

“The Knowledge Base Engine stores operational source material covering what NHID-Clinical is, the five controls, Shadow Pilot delivery, platform integration, pricing, terminology, and FAQs. Search filters across titles, tags, and body content, enabling operators to locate the right source before drafting a response or conducting a review.”

## Call Evaluation Workbench

“The Call Evaluation Workbench structures transcript assessment around the five policy controls. Each report card uses only the A, B, C, and F grading scale. The policy engine produces explainable checks for disclosure timing, PHI-request timing, deceptive-artifact language, escalation handling, and audit-trail completeness. Operators can export evaluated results for reporting and review.”

## Certification Tracker

“The Certification Tracker manages trainees across modules 101 through 107. The dashboard shows progress, assessment status, and the evidence used to support a certification outcome. It is designed to become the internal operating point for a future training program.”

## Content Calendar and Intelligence

“The content calendar uses the exact workflow Idea, Draft, Scheduled, and Published. It tracks copy, hashtags, platform, and publication timing across a 30-day operating horizon.”

“The competitive intelligence workspace focuses on Hyro, Orbita, Credo AI, and Arthur AI. Each profile carries a concise operating profile and positioning note, while the sales cheat sheet provides approved talking points for live conversations.”

## Administration and Close

“Finally, Settings presents the exact access levels: Admin, Consultant, and Partner. The Partner role is view-only for its own data. Organization configuration is also maintained here, with Stripe clearly labeled **Coming Soon** until production packaging is finalized.”

“That concludes the walkthrough. Shadow Pilot CRM provides a single operating surface for controlled delivery, evidence, communications, and ongoing readiness management.”
